Our Stories

The Daily Beacon was founded in 1998 by a small group of local reporters who believed our city deserved a newspaper that put community first. What began as a four-page weekly printed in a converted garage has grown into a daily source of news trusted by thousands of readers.

Over the years, we have covered city elections, school board decisions, local sports championships, and everything in between. Through every change in how people get their news, one thing has stayed the same: our commitment to accurate, fair reporting.

Today, The Daily Beacon is read online by readers across the region, but our newsroom is still just a short walk from where it all started, right here downtown.
